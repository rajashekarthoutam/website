About

I am an engineer driven by the boundary where digital logic synthesis meets low-level embedded hardware. Rather than relying solely on software-level access control, my work centers on designing deterministic hardware state machines in Verilog HDL, verifying RTL timing, and architecting physically isolated systems that prevent remote network exploits by design. Outside the lab, I design custom multi-layer PCBs in KiCad, probe signal lines on digital oscilloscopes, and assemble prototype electronics.
