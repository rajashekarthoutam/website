FPGA • HARDWARE ROOT OF TRUST • VERILOG HDL 2026

Secure Dual-Hardware Locking System

An air-gapped physical firewall architecture that physically decouples wireless RF communication from lock actuation logic. A Sipeed Tang Nano 20K FPGA acts as an immutable Hardware Root of Trust executing a cycle-accurate 16-bit Galois LFSR (polynomial x^16 + x^14 + x^13 + x^11 + 1) generating 65,535 non-repeating dynamic OTP codes rotated every 60 seconds. Wireless transmission is handled by an ESP32 running router-less ESP-NOW connected to the FPGA via a strictly unidirectional Simplex Optical UART link, guaranteeing 100% immunity to remote buffer overflows and replay attacks.

POWER ELECTRONICS • REAL-TIME FIRMWARE • SILICON CARBIDE 2026

Smart Bidirectional Double-Pole SiC Solid-State Circuit Breaker (SSCB)

A double-pole AC solid-state breaker replacing electromechanical inertia and contact arcing with 4× Wolfspeed C3M0075120K (1200V, 75mΩ) Silicon Carbide MOSFETs in common-source back-to-back pairs. Achieves an autonomous sub-microsecond trip path (<515–650 ns) via an STM32G431KB (ARM Cortex-M4F @ 170MHz) routing dual internal Analog Watchdogs (AWD1/AWD2) directly to Advanced Control Timer TIM1_BKIN break circuitry, bypassing software interrupt latency. Integrates coordinated MOV energy absorption (150 J), zero-crossing TRIAC dynamic PFC correction, and opto-isolated telemetry streaming True-RMS power over MQTT via an ESP32 FreeRTOS co-processor.

About

I am an engineer driven by the boundary where digital logic synthesis meets low-level embedded hardware and high-speed power electronics. Rather than relying solely on software-level access control or slow electromechanical mechanisms, my work centers on designing deterministic hardware state machines in Verilog HDL, autonomous sub-microsecond analog watchdog trip engines in modern ARM Cortex microcontrollers, and architecting physically isolated systems that prevent remote network exploits by design. Outside the lab, I design custom multi-layer PCBs in KiCad, probe high-voltage signal lines on digital oscilloscopes, and assemble prototype electronics.
